Date: 2026-09-05
Status: Prompt Ready — not rendered, not published

## Verified product surface

The connected AURELIS Base44 app currently includes:
- navigable Gallery and room routes,
- a Living Art experience,
- selected artworks with motion/video counterparts,
- current exhibition metadata,
- Seraphina concierge surfaces,
- a visitor-facing "Art That Lives. A Gallery That Never Sleeps." positioning line.

## Campaign concept

**Hook:** Some art hangs still. Some art waits for you to press play.

**Message:** AURELIS is not presented as an endless social feed. It is a digital art-house experience with rooms, exhibitions, selected Living Art works, and a concierge who can guide the visitor through the house.

**Short-form concept:**
1. Enter the gallery.
2. Move through a quiet room.
3. Stop at a selected Living Art work.
4. Press play and reveal its motion counterpart.
5. Close on: **AURELIS — Press play on the gallery.**

**CTA:** Enter the exhibition and explore Living Art.

## Claims boundary

Do not imply every artwork has a video counterpart. Do not claim real-world physical exhibition history unless separately verified. Avoid marketing language that names or imitates a living artist's protected style; describe technique and medium instead.
