## Campaign

**Hook:** Enter the room before the checkout.

Most online art commerce starts with a thumbnail and a price.

AURELIS is exploring a different sequence:

**cinematic entrance → gallery room → artwork → living-art context → concierge guidance → room preview → purchase decision**

The product idea is not simply to put art on another product grid. It is to make the experience of discovering and imagining the work part of the value.

Walk the gallery.
Ask Seraphina about what you are seeing.
Explore the room around the work.
Then decide whether the commerce layer deserves to come next.

**CTA:** Explore the gallery demo and identify the interaction you would want before buying art online.
