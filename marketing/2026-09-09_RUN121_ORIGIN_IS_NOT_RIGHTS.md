## Customer Truth
Origin evidence is not the same as commercial rights.

For AI-assisted or AI-generated art, a provenance marker can help document where a file or work came from, but it does not by itself establish ownership, copyright status, licensing authority, exclusivity, edition status, availability, or the right to use the work commercially.

## Creative Strategy
**Hook:** Verify where the work came from—and separately verify who can authorize the use.

**CTA:** Use the Origin & Rights Separation Card before a private acquisition or licensing review.
