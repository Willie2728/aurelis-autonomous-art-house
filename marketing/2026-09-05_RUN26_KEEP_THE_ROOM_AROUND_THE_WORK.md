## Campaign

**Hook:** A collector should not lose the gallery the moment they open the artwork.

Most commerce flows flatten a work into a product tile, a price, and a button.

AURELIS has a more differentiated path available in the connected product:

`room atmosphere → artwork → curatorial context → Living Art / still work → configuration → room preview / concierge → purchase decision`

The conversion idea is not to rush the buyer out of the room. It is to keep enough context around the work that the buyer can understand what they are considering before configuration or checkout.

For one featured piece, bring the collector in through its gallery room, preserve the curatorial statement, let them explore the relevant format, use Room Preview or Seraphina if helpful, and only then move toward acquisition.

**CTA:** Open one work from its room and explore it in context before you configure it.
