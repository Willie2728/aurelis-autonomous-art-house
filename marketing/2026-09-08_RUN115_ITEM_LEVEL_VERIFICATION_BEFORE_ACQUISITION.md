# AURELIS RUN115 — Item-Level Verification Before Acquisition

## Customer Truth
An attractive catalog image and a prototype price do not make an artwork acquisition-ready. A qualified collector conversation needs the item-level unknowns that matter to the intended use to be explicit before contact: identity/provenance, rights/edition, availability/final terms, fulfillment/installation, and the primary blocker.

## Creative Strategy
**AUR-DOC-004 — Artwork Verification Request**

Hook: **Before acquisition, verify the work—not just the image.**

The buyer aid asks:
1. What is verified about identity, creator/AI-generated status, date, medium, dimensions, and provenance?
2. What rights and edition terms actually apply?
3. Is the exact work available and what are the final commercial terms?
4. What physical/digital fulfillment, shipping, insurance, installation, display, or maintenance dependencies apply?
5. Which unresolved item must be verified before a viewing, acquisition, commission, or licensing conversation is worth scheduling?
