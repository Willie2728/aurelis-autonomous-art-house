## Customer Truth
A fictional studio persona can carry an aesthetic point of view. It should not borrow prestige from invented real-world education, museum holdings, awards, collectors, or gallery representation.

## Creative Strategy
**Hook:** Sell the work. Label the origin. Do not invent the artist résumé.

AURELIS can make the work easier to understand without pretending its Studio Personas have real-world careers they never had.

Lead with what the buyer can actually inspect: the image, medium/style language, origin label, edition/catalog status, price, and whether a motion asset genuinely exists. Keep the persona clearly fictional and technique-inspired. Let the work carry the commercial story.

**CTA:** Explore the catalog by work, style, medium, and clearly labeled origin.
